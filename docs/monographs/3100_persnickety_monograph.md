- **Evaluated Evidence Base**: 63 primary-source and lexicographical records (`CERT-3100-101` through `CERT-3100-416`), 63 pre-1931 composite WebP proof plates hosted in R2 (`62` `agent_verified` via independent blind Red-Team scan audit, `1` undated pamphlet retained at `discovered`).

## 1. Executive Synthesis & Prior-Art Calibration

North American *persnickety* ('overly particular, fastidious, fussy; snippy or aloof') represents a rhotic American sibilant reshaping of Lowland Scots [*pernickety, adj.*](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/pernickety_adj) ([*DSL/SND pernicketie*](https://dsl.ac.uk/entry/snd/pernicketie)). Against current scholarly baselines—**1808** in John Jamieson for Scots *pernickety*, **1885** in the Canton, Ohio *Repository* for American [*persnickety, adj. (& adv.)*](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/persnickety_adj), and **1913** in *The Rock Island Argus* for [*persnicketiness, n.*](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/persnicketiness_n)—this investigation establishes six documentary advances and calibrations:

1. **Elimination of the Modern `"1808 pernicktie"` Ghost (`CERT-3100-304`)**: First-edition scan inspection of [Jamieson’s *Etymological Dictionary*, Vol. II (1808), sig. 3G2r](https://archive.org/details/etymologicaldict02jami/page/n212/mode/1up) proves Jamieson printed four-syllable **`PERNICKITIE`** in 1808, correcting modern three-syllable `"pernicktie"` summaries and pushing four-syllable `PERNICKITIE` seventeen years earlier than *SND*'s 1825 *Supplement* citation.
2. **First-Edition Scots Orthography & 1822 Transatlantic Entry (`CERT-3100-305`, `306`, `313`)**: [John Galt’s January 1822 first edition of *Sir Andrew Wylie*, Vol. III, p. 181](https://archive.org/details/sirandrewwyliet07galtgoog/page/n188/mode/1up) prints velarized plural **`perknicketties`**, demonstrating early speaker association with *knick* / *knick-knack*, while Galt’s [May 1822 New York J. & J. Harper reprint of *The Provost*, p. 153](https://archive.org/details/provost00galtgoog/page/n157/mode/1up) put `per-nicketty` into American print in 1822. Collating [Froude (1882, I:31 `pernicked`)](https://archive.org/details/thomascarlyle01frou/page/31/mode/1up) against [Masson (*Macmillan’s*, Nov. 1881, p. 72 `per-nickety`)](https://archive.org/details/macmillansmagaz43grovgoog/page/n88/mode/1up) resolves the 1814 Carlyle citation divergence between *OED1* and *SND*.
3. **The 1834–1880 American Non-Sibilant Bridge to Stark County’s Border (`CERT-3100-101`..`105`)**: Non-sibilant `pernickity`/`pernicketty` circulated in American print from [Seba Smith’s *Life of Andrew Jackson* (Philadelphia, 1834, pp. 66, 78)](https://archive.org/details/lifeofandrewjack00smit/page/n81/mode/1up) through [*Putnam’s Magazine* (May 1870)](https://archive.org/details/sim_putnams-magazine_1870-05_5_29/page/n28/mode/1up), [*New-York Daily Tribune* (Sept. 15, 1870)](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030214/1870-09-15/ed-1/?sp=2), [*Cincinnati Daily Star* (Nov. 10, 1879)](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85025759/1879-11-10/ed-1/?sp=2), and Canfield’s [*Mahoning Dispatch* (Sept. 17, 1880, p. 1)](https://ohiomemory.org/digital/collection/p16007coll62/id/1387/) directly bordering Stark County, Ohio.
4. **Two Open-Scan 1885 Sibilant Witnesses & 1889–1892 Syndication (`CERT-3100-106`..`112`)**: While 1885 *Canton Repository* reels remain offline, open scans confirm sibilant `persnickity` on **September 22, 1885** in [*The Boston Weekly Globe* (`[Chicago Herald]`, p. 5)](https://archive.org/details/boston-weekly-globe-1885-09-22/page/n4/mode/1up) and `persnickerty` in Wooster, Ohio native Metta V. F. Victor’s **October 1885** [*The Bad Boy at Home* (p. 110, composed before June 26, 1885)](https://archive.org/details/badboyathomeand00victgoog/page/n112/mode/1up), followed by `persnickity` in [*Forest and Stream* (Aug. 1, 1889)](https://archive.org/details/ForeststreamXXXIB/page/n36/mode/1up), standard `persnickety` in [*Good Housekeeping* (Oct. 26, 1889)](https://archive.org/details/sim_good-housekeeping_good-housekeeping_1889-10-26_9_13/page/n9/mode/1up), and nationwide 1892 newspaper syndication.
5. **Word-Family Chronology & Reverse Export to Scotland (`CERT-3100-201`..`215`)**: Non-sibilant `pernicketyness` is antedated to **August 3, 1888** in [*The Engineer* (p. 100)](https://archive.org/details/sim_engineer_july-06-december-28-1888_66/page/100/mode/1up) (twelve years before *SND*'s 1900 *Spectator* citation) and `pernicketiness` to **1897** in [NEA *Proceedings* (p. 846)](https://archive.org/details/bwb_P9-BJI-412/page/846/mode/1up); OED’s **February 17, 1913** [*Rock Island Argus*](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn92053934/1913-02-17/ed-1/?sp=6) baseline for `persnicketiness` is verified on scan and traced to Cleveland columnist Mrs. Alice Gitchell Kirk; synthetic superlative `persnicketiest` is verified on **June 12, 1923** in Mary O’Connor Newell’s [*Chicago Daily Tribune* column (p. 19)](https://archive.org/details/per_chicago-daily-tribune_1923-06-12_82_140/page/n18/mode/1up); and Lorna Moon’s [*Century Magazine* story (July 1922, p. 386)](https://archive.org/details/centuryapopular06projgoog/page/n397/mode/1up) proves *SND*'s 1926 Scottish `persnickety` witness was re-imported from American magazine prose.
6. **Dictionary Lag, Settlement Prosopography & Falsified Myths (`CERT-3100-401`..`416`)**: Ten dictionary scans from [Bartlett (1884)](https://archive.org/details/dictionaryofamer00bart_1/page/n513/mode/1up) through [*OED1* Vol. VII (1909)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.207496/page/n725/mode/1up) and [*Webster’s New International* (1909 plates)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.207990/page/n327/mode/1up) prove a 20-year lag to dialect registration ([Pound 1905](https://archive.org/details/dialectnotes03newhuoft/page/63/mode/1up)) and a 49-year lag to general unabridged inclusion (1934), while primary scans of [Cotgrave (1611)](https://archive.org/details/cotgrave1611goog/page/n196/mode/1up), [Littré (1873)](https://archive.org/details/dictionnairedel01litt/page/n673/mode/1up), [Brockett (1825)](https://archive.org/details/glossaryofnorthc00brocuoft/page/n193/mode/1up), and [*Harpers’ Latin Dictionary* (1899 [1879 plates])](https://archive.org/details/harperslatindict02lewi/page/n1366/mode/1up) falsify the French *par chiquet*, Latin *pernicitas*, and pre-1808 *finicky* derivations.
