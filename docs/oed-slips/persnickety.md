# OED Portal Submission & Calibration Dossier — `persnickety` / `pernickety` (`TGT-008`, Case 3100)

## Slip 1 — `pernickety, adj.` (1808 First-Edition Headword Form `PERNICKITIE` & 1822 First-Edition `perknicketties`)
- **Target OED Entry**: [pernickety, adj.](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/pernickety_adj)
- **Visual Verification**: [Jamieson Vol. II (1808, sig. 3G2r, leaf n212)](https://archive.org/details/etymologicaldict02jami/page/n212/mode/1up) · [Galt, *Sir Andrew Wylie*, Vol. III (1822, p. 181, leaf n188)](https://archive.org/details/sirandrewwyliet07galtgoog/page/n188/mode/1up) (`CERT-3100-304`, `CERT-3100-305`)
- **Year**: 1808 (and 1822)
- **Quotation**: `PERNICKITIE, adj. Precise in trifles; applied also to dress, denoting trimness, S. perjink synon.` (1808) | `"Surely we're no to stand on such perknicketties wi' the like o' Martha Docken and her oye"` (1822)
- **Bibliographical Details**: John Jamieson, *An Etymological Dictionary of the Scottish Language*, Vol. II (Edinburgh: Creech, Constable, & Blackwood, 1808), sig. 3G2r; John Galt, *Sir Andrew Wylie, of That Ilk*, 1st ed., Vol. III (Edinburgh: William Blackwood, 1822), ch. xxii, p. 181.
- **Editorial Note**: Confirms on physical ink that Jamieson's 1808 first edition already printed four-syllable `PERNICKITIE` (rather than three-syllable `pernicktie`), while Galt's January 1822 first edition printed velarized plural `perknicketties` prior to later reprint normalization.

- **Quotation**: `The governor is so absurdly sensitive that its "pernicketyness" was the cause of making a certain engine which it regulated run much worse than it ought, and this has made the variation of speed in the present test greater than it happens to be.`
