# OED & Historical Slang Submission Dossier: Case 3300 (`bogus`, `TGT-004`)

## Slip 1 — `bogus, n.1 & adj.` (Attributive / Adjectival Sense — 4-Year Antedating: 1835 vs. 1839)
- **Target Entry**: [Oxford English Dictionary (`bogus, n.1 & adj.`)](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/bogus_n1) (currently **1839**, C. M. Kirkland, *A New Home*, calibrated to 1st ed. ch. XXXII, p. 212 in `CERT-3300-114`); [Green's Dictionary of Slang (`bogus, n.` sense 2 & `bogus, adj.`)](https://greensdictofslang.com/entry/fiqmj3q) (currently **7 Dec. 1838** coin noun / **1839** adj.).
- **New Attestation (`CERT-3300-108`)**: **17 January 1835** (reporting a **30 December 1834** raid), *Working Man's Advocate* (New York, ex *Detroit Journal*), Vol. 6, No. 23, p. 3, col. 2 ([Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/sim_young-america_1835-01-17_6_23/page/n2/mode/1up)):
  > "With one of them, a blacksmith, was found a quantity of coin, the particular value of which will be understood if we designate it as "Bogus Money.""
- **Corroborating Pre-1839 Witnesses**: **15 June 1837** *Plainfield Union* (ex Hudson *Ohio Observer*, `"Bogus money"`, `CERT-3300-211`); **7 July 1837** copyright deposit, Robert McCracken, *Original Miscellaneous Poems*, 2nd ed. (Pontiac, MI, pp. 17–18, `"making bogus"`, `"Bogus money"`, and personal count noun `"now he is a BOGUS"`, `CERT-3300-109`); **1 Dec. 1837** *Star & Republican Banner* (Gettysburgh, PA, p. 2, `"is bogus, a term to express spurious coin"`, `CERT-3300-111`); **20 Nov. 1841** *American Masonic Register* (Albany, NY, p. 94, non-monetary `"a "bogus" gentleman"`, `CERT-3300-117`, >10 years ahead of OED's 1852 figurative adjective).
- **Note on HDAS's "c. 1798 *Band of Brothers*"**: Exposed in `CERT-3300-201..205` as Chapter XII, p. 113 of Jonathan Harrington Green's **1847** *The Secret Band of Brothers* (serialized July–Sept. 1846 in *The Casket*, with post-1819 `"Western District of Virginia"` and post-1834 `"Toronto"` anachronisms).

## Slip 2 — `bogus, v.` (Verbal Conversion — 142-Year Antedating vs. GDoS 1995)
- **Target Entry**: [Green's Dictionary of Slang (`bogus, v.`)](https://greensdictofslang.com/entry/ptgteoa) (currently **1995**, J. Sack, *Company C*, p. 5: `"bogused"`).
- **New Attestation 1 (`CERT-3300-403`)**: **27 October 1853**, *Daily Morning News* (Savannah, GA), Vol. IV, No. 241, p. 1, col. 1 ([Georgia Historic Newspapers scan](https://gahistoricnewspapers.galileo.usg.edu/lccn/sn82015886/1853-10-27/ed-1/seq-1/)):
  > "There must be no bogusing in building Railroads... This "playing bogus" is a dangerous game in anything, especially in Railroad enterprises."
- **New Attestation 2 (`CERT-3300-408`)**: **20 July 1873**, *The Daily Sun* (Columbus, GA), Vol. XVIII, No. 271, p. 1, col. 1 ([Georgia Historic Newspapers scan](https://gahistoricnewspapers.galileo.usg.edu/lccn/sn82014939/1873-07-20/ed-1/seq-1/)):
  > "He bogused and fled, leaving many mourning creditors." (Paired with Oct. 1881 *The Art Amateur*, p. 94: `"out-bogussing"`.)

## Slip 3 — `bogus, n.1` (Composing-Room Filler Copy Sense — 19-Year Antedating: 1855 vs. 1874)
- **Target Entry**: [Oxford English Dictionary (`bogus, n.1 & adj.`)](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/bogus_n1) (composing-room filler sense currently **1874**).
- **New Attestation (`CERT-3300-404`)**: **1855** [copyright 1854; LC deposit 29 Jan. 1855], James Parton, *The Life of Horace Greeley* (New York: Mason Brothers), pp. 394–395 ([Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/lifeofhoracegree01part/page/394/mode/1up)):
  > "Bogus minion" / "bogus-hook" in the *New-York Tribune* composing room (corroborated in J. L. Ringwalt's 1871 *American Encyclopaedia of Printing*, p. 368, quoting Oct. 29, 1867 NY Typographical Union No. 6 rules: `"When bogus copy is given out in lieu of standing time"`, `CERT-3300-407`).
